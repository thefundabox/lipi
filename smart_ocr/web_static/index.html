Turn any PDF into editable text, in हिन्दी and English

Scans, phone photos, faded photocopies or digital files. Tell Lipi what kind of document it is, and it tunes the OCR for that input. Then check every page side by side with the original.

  • Hindi, English and mixed pages. Also catches broken Hindi text layers (such as Kruti Dev) and re-reads them.
  • Private by default. Files stay on this machine unless you turn on Claude for hard pages.
  • Review, edit, export. Low-confidence pages are flagged. Download as Word, Markdown or plain text.
